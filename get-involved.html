All of our work is volunteer-powered. Here's how to join it.

🙌

Volunteer

Join a Share & Care filling event — scoop, funnel, stitch, and tag eye pillows alongside middle and high schoolers. Or join a Treats for Tails craft day making blankets and toys. No experience needed, and younger volunteers are welcome with a parent.

Ask about the next event →
🤝

Partner with us

Are you a cancer center, hospice, hospital unit, shelter, or rescue organization in the Bay Area? We deliver handmade comfort directly to care teams and animals in need. Tell us about your team and we'll plan a delivery together.

Start the conversation →
💌

Spread the word

Know a nursing team running on empty, or a shelter that could use blankets? Introductions are one of the most valuable gifts you can give a small nonprofit. Forward our story to someone who should know us.

Say hello →
A note on donations: online giving is coming in v2 — we're setting up proper payment processing for the foundation first. Until then, the most helpful contributions are volunteer hours, material donations (fabric, rice, craft supplies), and introductions to care teams and shelters.
