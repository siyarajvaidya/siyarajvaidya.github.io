Volunteer

Join a Share & Care filling event, scoop, funnel, stitch, and tag eye pillows alongside middle and high schoolers. Or help run a Treats for Tails collection drive in your neighborhood. No experience needed, and younger volunteers are welcome with a parent.

Partner with us

Are you a cancer center, hospice, hospital unit, shelter, or rescue organization in the Bay Area? We deliver handmade comfort to care teams, and collected essentials to animals in need. Tell us about your team and we'll plan a delivery together.

Donate

Fuel the next batch.

Every gift goes to materials: pillow shells, filling, fabric, and tags. We are an all-volunteer foundation, so nothing is spent on salaries. Donations are tax-deductible; we email a receipt for every gift.

$25

Covers materials for about 10 comfort eye pillows.

$50

Covers materials for about 25 comfort eye pillows.

$100

Covers materials for about 45 comfort eye pillows.

$215

Sponsors a full batch of 100 pillows, enough for a whole care team at one hospital or hospice.

Ways to give:
Zelle: send to connect.krf@gmail.com (Kindroots Foundation). Please add your name and email in the memo so we can send your tax receipt.
Check: payable to Kind Roots Foundation. Email us at connect.krf@gmail.com for the mailing address.
Materials: fabric, rice, polyfill, and craft supplies are always welcome. Email us to arrange a drop-off.
