Our mission

In Siya's own words.

The mission below is exactly as she wrote it at the start — the voice stays hers.

At Treats for Tails, we believe every pet deserves comfort and care, regardless of their circumstances. Our mission is to bring warmth and happiness to pets in need by providing blankets, toys, and other essentials.

Through community donations and dedicated volunteer efforts, we strive to make a difference one blanket, one toy, and one wagging tail at a time.

How it started

Summer 2023. One eighth grader, a pile of fabric.

Treats for Tails began in the summer of 2023, as Siya was finishing 8th grade. She started making blankets and toys for shelter animals — simple, handmade comfort for pets waiting for homes.

What began as one person's craft project grew through community donations and dedicated volunteer efforts into an ongoing program. Today it runs as a program of Kindroots Foundation, still volunteer-made and volunteer-delivered, still guided by the same belief: every pet deserves comfort and care.
