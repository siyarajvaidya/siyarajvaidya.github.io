What we make

Small things, big comfort.

Simple handmade goods that shelters actually need. Every item is made by a volunteer.

🛏️

Blankets

Cozy, washable blankets for kennels and foster homes. Soft bedding that makes a strange place feel a little more like home.

[ PHOTO: handmade blankets ]
🧸

Toys

Handmade toys that keep shelter pets playing. Because play is its own kind of care — and a busy pup is a happy pup.

[ PHOTO: handmade toys ]
🎁

Essentials

Supplies shelters request, gathered through community drives. The practical things that keep daily care running.

[ PHOTO: donated essentials ]
How it works

From our hands to their paws.

  • Volunteers make. Blankets and toys are handmade at craft days and at home.
  • Drives gather. Essentials come in through community supply drives.
  • We deliver. Everything goes directly to shelters and rescue organizations.
