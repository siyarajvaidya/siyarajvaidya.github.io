What we collect

Small things, big comfort.

Simple things shelters actually need, gathered through community drives and doorstep pickups.

🛏️

Blankets

Cozy, washable blankets for kennels and foster homes. Soft bedding that makes a strange place feel a little more like home.

🧸

Toys

Toys that keep shelter pets playing. Because play is its own kind of care, and a busy pup is a happy pup.

🎁

Essentials

Supplies shelters request, gathered through community drives. The practical things that keep daily care running.

How it works

From our hands to their paws.

  • Neighbors donate. Blankets, toys, and essentials come in through drives and doorstep pickups.
  • Volunteers sort. Every item is checked and bundled for the shelter that asked for it.
  • We deliver. Everything goes directly to shelters and rescue organizations.

Help us gather more.

Every delivery starts with someone willing to help. Donate a blanket or run a supply drive.
