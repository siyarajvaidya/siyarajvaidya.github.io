Join the pack.

There's a job for every pair of hands — and every skill level. No experience needed, just a soft spot for animals.

Volunteer

Join us for craft days, where we make blankets and toys together; help run supply drives; or come along on shelter deliveries and see the difference firsthand.

🎁

Donate supplies

We gather the essentials shelters request through community drives. Every donated item goes straight to animals in need — nothing in between.

Are you a shelter?

If you're a shelter or rescue organization, we'd love to hear what your animals need most. Tell us, and we'll see what our volunteers can make or gather.
