What we need

Supplies shelters actually need.

Treats for Tails collects donated pet supplies in our neighborhood and delivers them to local shelters. If you have any of the items below, we would love to pass them on to animals waiting for a home.

Donate these items

What we can accept

  • Unopened dry and wet dog and cat food
  • Unopened pet treats
  • Blankets and towels, new or gently used
  • Leashes and collars
  • Pet toys
  • Pee pads
Please note

What we cannot accept

  • Opened or expired food
  • Medications
  • Damaged items

Shelters can only use food that is sealed and in date, so please check labels before donating.
