News.

Milestones and updates from our programs, newest first. Every date below is real — including the ones from before this site existed.

October 3, 2026

Eye pillows delivered to Hospice of the East Bay

Comfort eye pillows delivered to the hospice clinical team in Pleasant Hill — our first hospice delivery. Each pillow was hand-sewn, rice-filled, and finished with the share & care tag. Thank you to every volunteer who sewed, filled, and tagged this batch.

September 2026

200 pillows prepped for our next volunteer filling event

Two hundred pillow shells are sewn and ready for filling. At our next volunteer event, middle and high schoolers will fill them with rice, stitch them shut, and attach the tags. Want to join? Get in touch →

November 17, 2025

IRS recognizes Kindroots as a 501(c)(3) public charity

Our determination letter arrived: Kindroots Foundation is officially a tax-exempt public charity, and donations are tax-deductible. Two years of kitchen-table volunteer work, now with a permanent home.

June 2025

Kindroots Foundation is incorporated

We're now a California nonprofit public benefit corporation. Incorporating gives our volunteer programs — Share & Care and Treats for Tails — a legal foundation to grow on.

Summer 2024

Share & Care begins

Finishing 9th grade, Siya starts a second handmade project alongside Treats for Tails: comfort gifts for cancer-care teams and the people who look after patients.

Summer 2023

Treats for Tails begins

Finishing 8th grade, Siya starts making blankets and toys for shelter animals. The first stitch in what would become Kindroots Foundation.
