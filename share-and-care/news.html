Deliveries, volunteer events, and milestones — posted as they happen, dated the day they happened. No backfill, no fiction.

October 3, 2026

Eye pillows delivered to Hospice of the East Bay clinical team

Our first hospice delivery is done. Comfort eye pillows — sewn, filled, and tagged by volunteers — were delivered in person to the clinical team at Hospice of the East Bay in Pleasant Hill.

Hospice teams do some of the hardest and most tender work in healthcare. We're honored our pillows will be resting over their tired eyes at the end of long days.

September 2026

200 pillows prepped — volunteer filling event coming

Some 200 pillow shells are sewn and ready for filling. We're planning a volunteer filling event where middle and high schoolers will scoop, stitch, tag, and pack every one of them.

Want in? Join the crew — no experience needed.
