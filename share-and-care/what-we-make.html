What we make

One pillow, one pause.

A comfort eye pillow is a small thing: soft cotton shell, rice or polyfill inside, a gentle weight over tired eyes. We fill and finish them by hand, in batches, and give them to the people who spend their days caring for everyone else.

Inside every pillow

Simple ingredients, honestly made.

  • Machine-sewn shells — soft cotton, sewn in India, funded by Siya's earnings from her part-time job.
  • Rice or polyfill — plain rice gives a gentle, even weight (and can be briefly chilled for a cooling rest); soft polyfill gives a lighter, plusher feel.
  • Tagged with care — every pillow carries its use instructions and our contact, so the gift keeps explaining itself.
  • For external use only — a small comfort, clearly labeled, made to be safe.

From fabric to doorstep

How a pillow gets made.

Four steps, many hands. Most of the work happens at our volunteer filling events.

1

Sew

Shells are machine-sewn in India, paid for by Siya's earnings from her part-time job — each with an opening left for filling.

2

Fill

At filling events, volunteers fill each shell with plain rice or soft polyfill — the gentle weight that makes the pillow work.

3

Tag

Shells are stitched shut and finished with our tag: care instructions on the back, our promise on the front.

4

Deliver

Pillows are packed up and delivered in person to clinical teams — never just shipped and forgotten.
