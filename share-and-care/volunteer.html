Volunteer

Many hands, light work.

Our filling events turn an ordinary afternoon into a small factory of comfort. Come for an hour or stay all day — every pillow you touch ends up resting over someone's tired eyes.

Pick your station

Three jobs, zero experience needed.

At a filling event, everyone rotates through the stations. Here's what each one involves.

🫙

Fill

Scoop plain rice or soft polyfill and funnel it into pre-sewn pillow shells. It's the busiest station and the most satisfying — you can feel each pillow take its shape.

🪡

Finish

Stitch the filled shells shut and attach the tags. Basic hand-stitching is taught on the spot; if you can thread a needle, you can do this.

💌

Encourage

Write the note that goes out with the pillows. A few honest lines about rest mean more than you think to someone ending a twelve-hour shift.

Who can join

Built for students.

  • Middle and high schoolers — our crews are students, start to finish.
  • No experience needed — every skill is taught at the event.
  • All supplies provided — fabric, rice, polyfill, funnels, needles, tags. Just bring yourself.
  • Service hours — we log volunteer hours for school requirements on request.
