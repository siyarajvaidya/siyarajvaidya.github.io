What we make

One pillow, one pause.

Each eye pillow is filled with rice or soft polyfill, and finished with our tag: care instructions on the back, our promise on the front.

share & care

Recreated from the real printed tag attached to every pillow.

  • Soft cotton shells, filled, stitched shut, and tagged by teen volunteers at our filling events.
  • Rice or polyfill, plain rice for a gentle weight, or soft polyfill for a lighter rest.
  • Tagged with care, every pillow carries use instructions and our contact.
  • Delivered in person, to the clinical teams who care for others all day.
