Who we help

For the people who never clock out.

Nurses, hospice teams, and cancer-care staff carry everyone else's hardest days. Our pillows go to them — the caregivers behind the care.

Our story

It started with an afternoon of sewing.

Share & Care began in the summer of 2024, founded by Siya Rajvaidya as a simple idea: the people who comfort patients all day deserve a little comfort themselves. What started as handmade gifts made at home is now a program of Kindroots Foundation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit — with volunteer crews, filling events, and deliveries across the Bay Area.

October 3, 2026

Hospice of the East Bay

Comfort eye pillows delivered in person to the clinical team in Pleasant Hill — our first hospice delivery. Each pillow went out tagged and ready to rest over tired eyes at the end of a long shift.
