Who we help

For the people who never clock out.

Nurses, hospice teams, and cancer-care staff carry everyone else's hardest days. Our pillows go to them, the caregivers behind the care.

Our story

It started with an afternoon of sewing.

Share & Care began with an idea in November 2024: the people who comfort patients all day deserve a little comfort themselves. After a few months of trying different ways to make the pillows, Siya made the first delivery in February 2025, to Suncrest Hospice in Fremont. It is now a program of Kindroots Foundation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, with volunteer crews, filling events, and deliveries across the Bay Area.
