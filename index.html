Our programs

Two programs, one root system.

Each program runs its own site and volunteer crew. Kindroots is the trunk that holds them up: the legal nonprofit, the shared values, the story.

🌙

Share & Care

Handmade comfort eye pillows for cancer-care and hospice teams. Made by teen volunteers, delivered with a note that says rest a little — you deserve it too.
